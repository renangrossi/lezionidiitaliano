Coesione Testuale Avanzata: Anafora ed Ellissi

Keeping a long text connected without repeating yourself.

ObiettiviSpiegazioneRegoleEsempiErrori ComuniPraticaRiepilogoContinua

Introduzione

A cohesive text avoids repeating the same noun sentence after sentence, but also avoids ambiguity about what each pronoun refers back to — a balance advanced writing has to manage deliberately.

Al termine di questa lezione saprai…

  • Use anaphoric reference (pronouns, demonstratives) to avoid repetition across sentences
  • Recognize and use ellipsis to omit a repeated element cleanly
  • Maintain clarity of reference across a multi-sentence paragraph

Spiegazione

Spiegazione

Anaphora here means referring back to something already mentioned, using a pronoun, demonstrative (questo/quello), or a summarizing noun (tale scelta, questo fatto) instead of repeating it. Ellipsis is the clean omission of a repeated element when the grammar makes it recoverable: Marco studia legge, Anna [studia] medicina.

Regole Grammaticali

Le Regole

a) Anaphoric devices

  • Pronouns: lo, la, li, le, ne, ci
  • Demonstratives: questo, quello, ciò (ciò is a fixed, invariable "this/that [abstract idea]")
  • Summarizing nouns: tale decisione, questo approccio, il tutto

b) Ellipsis

Omitting a repeated verb (or verb phrase) when context makes it recoverable: Lui ama la musica classica, lei [ama] il jazz.

c) Avoiding ambiguous reference

When two possible antecedents exist, prefer a demonstrative or a repeated noun over an ambiguous pronoun: Marco ha parlato con Luca; quest'ultimo sembrava preoccupato. (quest'ultimo = "the latter", unambiguous.)

Esempi

Vediamola in Pratica

  • Il progetto è ambizioso. Ciò non significa che sia irrealizzabile.
    The project is ambitious. That doesn't mean it's unachievable.
  • Marco preferisce il tè, Anna il caffè.
    Marco prefers tea, Anna [prefers] coffee. (ellipsis)
  • Ho parlato con il direttore e con il suo assistente; quest'ultimo si è mostrato molto disponibile.
    I spoke with the director and his assistant; the latter was very helpful. (unambiguous anaphora)
  • Tale scelta ha sorpreso molti osservatori.
    This choice surprised many observers. (summarizing anaphora)

Errori Comuni

Attenzione a

EvitaRepeating the full noun phrase in every sentence of a paragraph

Usa inveceVary with pronouns, demonstratives, and summarizing nouns

Constant repetition of the same full noun phrase reads as mechanical and unpolished in fluent written Italian.

EvitaUsing lui/lei ambiguously when two people of the same gender were just mentioned

Usa inveceUse quest'ultimo/quel primo or repeat the name for clarity

An ambiguous pronoun with two possible antecedents forces the reader to guess — advanced writing resolves this explicitly.

Riepilogo

Ripasso

  • Anaphoric devices (pronouns, ciò, quest'ultimo, summarizing nouns) avoid repeating a noun phrase across sentences.
  • Ellipsis cleanly omits a repeated verb/phrase when grammar makes it recoverable.
  • Ambiguous pronoun reference is resolved with demonstratives like quest'ultimo, not left to guesswork.
