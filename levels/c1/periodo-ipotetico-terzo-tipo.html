Il Periodo Ipotetico di Terzo Tipo

"If I had known..." — the impossible past.

ObiettiviSpiegazioneRegoleEsempiErrori ComuniPraticaRiepilogoContinua

Introduzione

The third type of hypothetical sentence talks about something that didn't happen in the past — and, by logical necessity, whose consequence also never happened.

Al termine di questa lezione saprai…

  • Form the third type hypothetical sentence for the unreal past
  • Use the condizionale passato in the main clause
  • Recognize mixed hypotheticals (past condition, present consequence)

Spiegazione

Spiegazione

Se + congiuntivo trapassato, condizionale passato (avrei/sarei + past participle): Se avessi saputo, sarei venuto. (If I had known, I would have come — but I didn't know, and I didn't come.) Both halves describe a counterfactual past.

Regole Grammaticali

Le Regole

a) Formation

se + avessi/fossi + participle, avrei/sarei + participle: Se avessi studiato di più, avrei passato l'esame.

b) Mixed hypothetical

A past condition can have a present consequence: Se avessi accettato quel lavoro, ora vivrei a Milano. (If I had accepted that job, I'd be living in Milan now.) — condizionale presente in the main clause, since the consequence is felt now, not back then.

Esempi

Vediamola in Pratica

  • Se avessi saputo, ti avrei chiamato subito.
    If I had known, I would have called you right away.
  • Se non avessimo perso il treno, saremmo già arrivati.
    If we hadn't missed the train, we would already have arrived.
  • Avrei accettato l'offerta se me l'avessero fatta prima.
    I would have accepted the offer if they had made it to me sooner.
  • Se mi fossi laureato in ingegneria, ora lavorerei in un'altra azienda.
    If I had graduated in engineering, I'd be working at a different company now. (mixed)

Errori Comuni

Attenzione a

EvitaSe avrei saputo, sarei venuto.

Usa inveceSe avessi saputo, sarei venuto.

The se-clause needs the congiuntivo trapassato, never the conditional — same rule as the second type, one tense further back.

EvitaSe avessi studiato di più, avessi passato l'esame.

Usa inveceSe avessi studiato di più, avrei passato l'esame.

The main clause needs the condizionale passato, not a repeated subjunctive.

Riepilogo

Ripasso

  • Third type (unreal past): se + congiuntivo trapassato, condizionale passato.
  • Both halves are counterfactual: neither the condition nor its consequence actually happened.
  • A mixed hypothetical pairs a past condition with a condizionale presente for a present consequence.
