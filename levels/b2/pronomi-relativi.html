I Pronomi Relativi

Che, cui, il quale — linking two ideas about the same noun.

ObjectivesExplanationRulesExamplesCommon MistakesPracticeSummaryRelated

Introduction

Relative pronouns let you fold a second sentence about the same noun directly into the first, instead of starting a new one — the backbone of fluent, connected Italian.

By the end of this lesson you can…

  • Use che as the all-purpose relative pronoun for subject and direct object
  • Use cui after a preposition
  • Use il quale/la quale as a more formal, gender-marked alternative to cui

Explanation

Explanation

Che is invariable and covers both subject and direct object relative clauses — by far the most common case. As soon as a preposition is involved, che is replaced by cui (also invariable), with the preposition placed directly before it. Il quale/la quale/i quali/le quali is a more formal alternative to cui, useful mainly when cui's lack of gender/number would otherwise be ambiguous.

Grammar Rules

The Rules

a) Che — subject or direct object

La ragazza che parla è mia sorella. (subject) Il libro che ho letto è bellissimo. (direct object)

b) Cui — after a preposition

La persona a cui ho scritto (the person I wrote to) Il paese in cui sono nato (the country I was born in) di cui parlava = what he was talking about

c) Cui with an article = whose

il cui, la cui, i cui, le cui (agreeing with the noun that follows, not with the owner): Lo scrittore il cui libro ho letto. (The writer whose book I read.)

Examples

See It in Action

  • Il film che abbiamo visto ieri era fantastico.
    The film we watched yesterday was fantastic.
  • La città in cui sono cresciuto è piccola.
    The city I grew up in is small.
  • Ecco la persona di cui ti parlavo.
    Here's the person I was telling you about.
  • L'uomo con cui lavoro è molto simpatico.
    The man I work with is very nice.
  • Lo scrittore il cui romanzo ha vinto il premio è italiano.
    The writer whose novel won the prize is Italian.

Common Mistakes

Watch Out For

AvoidLa persona a che ho scritto.

Use insteadLa persona a cui ho scritto.

After a preposition, che is replaced by cui — che alone never follows a preposition.

AvoidIl cui il libro (word order)

Use insteadil cui libro

Cui + article goes directly before the noun it modifies, with no extra article repeated.

Summary

Review

  • Che covers subject and direct object relative clauses — the default, most common choice.
  • Cui is used directly after any preposition, and is invariable.
  • Il/la/i/le cui = whose, agreeing with the noun possessed, not the owner.
