Domande e Parole Interrogative

Ask yes/no questions and use the question words: chi, cosa, dove, quando, perché, come, quanto.

ObiettiviSpiegazioneRegoleEsempiErrori ComuniPraticaRiepilogoContinua

Introduzione

Forming a question in Italian is refreshingly simple: for yes/no questions, the word order usually doesn't change at all.

Al termine di questa lezione saprai…

  • Form a yes/no question with no word-order change
  • Use the seven core question words
  • Answer perché questions with perché

Spiegazione

Spiegazione

A statement becomes a yes/no question just by using rising intonation in speech, or a question mark in writing — Parli italiano. becomes Parli italiano? with no other change. For questions asking for information, place the question word at the start of the sentence.

Regole Grammaticali

Le Regole

a) Yes/no questions

Same word order as the statement, just rising intonation / a question mark: Sei italiano? Hai fame? Vuoi un caffè?

b) The seven question words

  • chi — who: Chi è?
  • cosa / che cosa — what: Cosa fai?
  • dove — where: Dove abiti?
  • quando — when: Quando parti?
  • perché — why: Perché studi l'italiano?
  • come — how: Come stai?
  • quanto/a/i/e — how much/many (agrees like an adjective): Quanti anni hai?

c) Answering perché

Perché means both "why" and "because" — context (and a comma, in writing) tells them apart: Perché studi l'italiano? Perché mi piace. (Why do you study Italian? Because I like it.)

Esempi

Vediamola in Pratica

  • Dove abiti?
    Where do you live?
  • Quando arriva il treno?
    When does the train arrive?
  • Perché sei triste? Perché ho perso il lavoro.
    Why are you sad? Because I lost my job.
  • Quanti fratelli hai?
    How many siblings do you have?
  • Parli spagnolo?
    Do you speak Spanish?
  • Come si dice "grazie" in inglese?
    How do you say "thank you" in English?

Errori Comuni

Attenzione a

EvitaFai cosa?

Usa inveceCosa fai? / Che cosa fai?

Question words go at the start of the sentence, not at the end as in some casual English word order.

EvitaQuanto sorelle hai?

Usa inveceQuante sorelle hai?

Quanto agrees like an adjective — sorelle is feminine plural, so it must be quante.

EvitaDo you speak Spanish? — Fai tu parlare spagnolo?

Usa inveceParli spagnolo?

Italian doesn't use a "do/does" auxiliary for questions — just use the plain conjugated verb.

Riepilogo

Ripasso

  • Yes/no questions in Italian use the same word order as the statement — only intonation/punctuation changes.
  • The seven core question words: chi, cosa, dove, quando, perché, come, quanto (which agrees like an adjective).
  • Perché means both "why" and "because" — the same word covers both sides of the exchange.
