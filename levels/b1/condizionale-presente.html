Il Condizionale Presente

Would — for politeness, wishes, and advice.

ObjectivesExplanationRulesExamplesCommon MistakesPracticeSummaryRelated

Introduction

The conditional is what makes Italian sound polite: vorrei instead of voglio, potrebbe instead of può — the single grammatical feature that softens a request into a courtesy.

By the end of this lesson you can…

  • Conjugate regular verbs in the condizionale presente
  • Use vorrei for polite requests
  • Use the conditional of dovere for advice

Explanation

Explanation

The conditional shares the same irregular stems as the future (sar-, avr-, andr-, and so on) but takes its own distinct set of endings. It's used constantly in everyday speech for politeness, wishes, and giving advice.

Grammar Rules

The Rules

a) Endings

-ei, -esti, -ebbe, -emmo, -este, -ebbero, added to the same stem as the future (parler-, avr-, sar-...).

b) Politeness

Vorrei (I would like) is the standard polite way to order or request, replacing the blunter voglio: Vorrei un caffè, per favore. Potrebbe aiutarmi? (Could you help me? — formal)

c) Advice with dovrei/dovresti

Dovresti riposare di più. (You should rest more.) Softer and more natural than the blunt obligation of devi.

Examples

See It in Action

  • Vorrei un tavolo per due, per favore.
    I would like a table for two, please.
  • Mi piacerebbe visitare la Sicilia.
    I would love to visit Sicily.
  • Dovresti parlare con lui.
    You should talk to him.
  • Potresti chiudere la finestra?
    Could you close the window?
  • Sarebbe fantastico viaggiare insieme.
    It would be fantastic to travel together.

Common Mistakes

Watch Out For

AvoidVoglio un caffè. (as a polite order in a café)

Use insteadVorrei un caffè, per favore.

Voglio is grammatically fine but blunt — vorrei is the standard polite register for ordering or requesting.

AvoidDovrei che tu studi di più.

Use insteadDovresti studiare di più.

Dovere + infinitive needs no conjunction che — it pairs directly with the infinitive, just like in the present tense.

AvoidIo vorrei andare al mare. spelled voglierei

Use insteadvorrei (irregular stem vorr-, not a regular volere stem)

Volere has an irregular conditional stem, vorr-, shared with the future — not a regular form built from the infinitive.

Summary

Review

  • The conditional shares the future's irregular stems, with its own endings: -ei, -esti, -ebbe, -emmo, -este, -ebbero.
  • Vorrei/potrebbe soften requests into polite register — used constantly in daily life.
  • Dovrei/dovresti give gentle advice, softer than the direct obligation of devo/devi.
