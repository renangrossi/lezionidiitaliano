L'Imperfetto

Italian's other past tense — for background, habits, and descriptions.

ObiettiviSpiegazioneRegoleEsempiErrori ComuniPraticaRiepilogoContinua

Introduzione

While the passato prossimo reports a completed event, the imperfetto paints the background: what things were like, what used to happen, what was already going on.

Al termine di questa lezione saprai…

  • Conjugate regular verbs in the imperfetto
  • Conjugate the three key irregular imperfetto verbs: essere, fare, bere
  • Use the imperfetto for habitual past actions and descriptions

Spiegazione

Spiegazione

The imperfetto is one of the most regular tenses in Italian — nearly every verb (even most that are irregular elsewhere) follows the same pattern, built by dropping -re from the infinitive and adding the imperfetto endings. Only essere is truly irregular; fare and bere use their old Latin stems (facere, bevere) rather than the modern infinitive.

Regole Grammaticali

Le Regole

a) Regular pattern

Drop -are/-ere/-ire, add: -vo, -vi, -va, -vamo, -vate, -vano (with the conjugation's vowel kept: parla-vo, cred-e-vo, dorm-i-vo).

b) When to use the imperfetto

  • Habitual/repeated past actions: Da bambino giocavo sempre fuori. (As a child I always used to play outside.)
  • Descriptions (people, places, weather, time, age) in the past: Era una bella giornata. Faceva caldo.
  • Background action interrupted by another (paired with passato prossimo, next lesson): Dormivo quando hai chiamato.

Esempi

Vediamola in Pratica

  • Da piccolo abitavo in campagna.
    As a child I lived in the countryside.
  • Ogni estate andavamo al mare.
    Every summer we would go to the seaside.
  • Erano le nove di sera.
    It was nine in the evening.
  • Faceva molto freddo quel giorno.
    It was very cold that day.
  • Mia nonna raccontava sempre storie bellissime.
    My grandmother always used to tell wonderful stories.

Errori Comuni

Attenzione a

EvitaIeri sono andato al mare ogni giorno.

Usa inveceOgni giorno andavo al mare.

A repeated/habitual action needs the imperfetto, not the passato prossimo, which reports a single completed event.

EvitaIo ero avevo dieci anni.

Usa inveceAvevo dieci anni.

Age uses avere alone (avevo), not combined with essere.

EvitaFecevo i compiti ogni sera. (for fare)

Usa inveceFacevo i compiti ogni sera.

Fare's imperfetto stem is fac- (from facere), giving facevo, not a form based on the modern infinitive fare.

Riepilogo

Ripasso

  • The imperfetto is built regularly: stem + -vo/-vi/-va/-vamo/-vate/-vano; only essere is truly irregular.
  • Use it for habitual past actions, descriptions, and background states.
  • Contrast with the passato prossimo, which reports single, completed events — next lesson.
