The Framework & Your Path Through It

About the CEFR

The Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR) is the international standard for describing language ability. It organizes this course into six levels — enter any level below.

  1. A1

    Beginner

    Basic phrases and everyday expressions for immediate needs. Enter the level

  2. A2

    Elementary

    Simple, direct exchanges on familiar topics and routine matters. Enter the level

  3. B1

    Intermediate

    Independent use of Italian for work, study and travel situations. Enter the level

  4. B2

    Upper Intermediate

    Fluent, spontaneous interaction and clear, detailed argument. Enter the level

  5. C1

    Advanced

    Flexible, effective language use for academic and professional life. Enter the level

  6. C2

    Proficient

    Precise, nuanced command of Italian in virtually any context. Enter the level
